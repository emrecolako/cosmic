# Cosmic Blueprint pricing

- The [fictional sample reading](https://unifiedreading.com/sample) is free and needs no personal information.
- A calculated personal profile and generated preview are available before checkout.
- A full personal reading is a one-time purchase when payments are enabled. There is no subscription or account requirement.
- The current price and whether checkout is enabled are shown on the [pricing page](https://unifiedreading.com/pricing). Stripe Checkout shows the final amount before payment.

The full reading contains a longer synthesis and practical takeaways. See [About](https://unifiedreading.com/about) for chart limitations and [Privacy](https://unifiedreading.com/privacy) for payment data handling.
