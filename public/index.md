# Cosmic Blueprint

Cosmic Blueprint is a web app for a combined, personal reflection reading. It connects Pythagorean numerology, Western zodiac signs, the Chinese zodiac, and the life stage a visitor selects. The reading highlights common themes and tensions and suggests practical ways to reflect on them.

## How it works

1. Enter a birth name and date. Birth time, birthplace, gender, and personal context are optional.
2. Choose the life stages that matter now.
3. Review the calculated profile and a generated interpretation preview. A one-time checkout for the full interpretation is shown when payments are enabled.

The public [fictional sample](https://unifiedreading.com/sample) requires no personal details. Numerology values, zodiac signs, and the Chinese zodiac are calculated in the browser. The interpretation is generated through OpenRouter model providers.

The chart is approximate and sign-level. Unknown birth time omits Moon, rising, and house claims. Houses, planetary aspects, and current transits are not calculated. This is an interpretive experience for reflection, not a scientific personality test or a prediction.
